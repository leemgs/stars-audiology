\section{Planned Analyses and Reproducible Scaffold}
\subsection{Planned primary analyses (no results reported)}
No epidemiological findings are reported in this protocol. Per the prespecified aims, the planned analyses will report: (i) weighted prevalence of tinnitus presence, bothersome tinnitus, and hearing impairment in KNHANES with 95\% CIs; (ii) primary (minimal-adjustment-set) associations between perceived stress and each endpoint, with the extended mediator-adjusted model reported separately; (iii) associations with better- and worse-ear and per-ear audiometric thresholds, expected to attenuate after adjusting for age and noise (H1); (iv) all-adult and, secondarily, employed-restricted analyses (healthy-worker check); (v) NHANES external validation reporting unweighted and weighted performance, calibration-in-the-large and slope before/after recalibration, common support, and case-mix-standardized performance (H2); and (vi) subgroup fairness including SES and race/ethnicity. Every table will separate public-data analyses from any future clinical-extension analyses.

\subsection{Reproducible scaffold demonstration (synthetic; not a result)}
To show that the pipeline runs end-to-end and produces correctly structured, TRIPOD+AI-aligned outputs, the repository ships a synthetic-data demonstration. \textbf{These numbers are artifacts of randomly generated data and carry no epidemiological meaning}; they exist only to verify wiring, metric computation, and reporting format, and are deliberately kept out of the abstract and any headline. On the synthetic sample the tinnitus target yields AUROC~$\approx$~0.64, AUPRC~$\approx$~0.49, Brier~$\approx$~0.20, and the hearing-loss target yields an analogous record (Table~\ref{tab:metrics}). Substituting real KNHANES/NHANES files following the documented layout routes the identical code path to the design-consistent estimates specified above.

\subsection{Planned AI-component reporting}
LLM-extracted fields will be reported with the per-field metric suite of Section~\ref{sec:ai} (exact match; precision/recall/F1; omission; span agreement; temporal/laterality/onset/duration accuracy; document-level match; run consistency; clinically significant error rate), plus clinician correction time and per-version performance. The red-flag layer will be reported per Table~\ref{tab:redflag} (dual-expert-labeled vignettes; sensitivity with 95\% CI; specificity/over-referral; subgroup and robustness), with any missed red flag audited as a critical failure and with the explicit caveat that finite-set perfection does not guarantee zero deployment misses. Model cards accompany each released model, documenting intended use (Table~\ref{tab:intended}), data provenance, subgroup metrics, and prohibitions.
