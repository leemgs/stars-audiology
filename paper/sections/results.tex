\section{Results}\label{sec:results}
This section reports the \emph{primary} perceived-stress analyses in the KNHANES
development cohort, followed by the \emph{supporting} NHANES analyses. KNHANES is
the primary test of the aims because, unlike NHANES, it carries a general
perceived-stress item (the NHANES psychological-distress proxy is the PHQ-9
depression screen, which the study DAG treats as a \emph{mediator}). All public-data
estimates are design-based (survey-weighted, Taylor-linearized) and are
associations, not causal effects.

\subsection{Primary results (KNHANES development cohort)}
The primary analyses use real KNHANES microdata for Korean adults aged 40--69,
pooling the otologic-examination cycles 2010--2012 (KNHANES V). The
perceived-stress and covariate items (health-interview file) were merged on the
person identifier to the separate ENT (otolaryngology) examination file that carries
pure-tone audiometry, the tinnitus item, and occupational-noise exposure; the
survey weight (\texttt{wt\_itvex}) was pooled across the three years and
Taylor-linearized. The analysis is produced by
\texttt{code/src/knhanes\_analysis.py} with \texttt{config/knhanes\_mapping.yaml}
(the same validated design-based estimators as the NHANES pipeline), and the
committed \texttt{outputs/knhanes\_results.json} reproduces every value below.
\IfFileExists{tables/table_results_knhanes.tex}{\input{tables/table_results_knhanes}}{}

\paragraph{Prevalence.} Survey-weighted prevalence among adults 40--69 was 21.1\%
(95\% CI 19.9--22.3; $n=9{,}657$) for any tinnitus, 7.0\% (6.3--7.7) for
bothersome tinnitus (moderate-or-severe annoyance on the KNHANES 3-level item,
$\approx$33\% of those with tinnitus), 15.4\% (14.5--16.4; $n=9{,}325$) for
better-ear speech-frequency hearing loss ($>$25 dB HL), and 24.3\% (23.2--25.3;
$n=9{,}751$) for high perceived stress.

\paragraph{Primary association (H1).} In the mutually adjusted, minimal-sufficient
model (perceived stress, occupational noise, age, sex), high perceived stress was
associated with \emph{tinnitus} (OR 1.42, 1.26--1.60), alongside occupational
noise (OR 1.37, 1.15--1.63) and age (OR 1.03 per year), with no significant sex
difference. Its association with \emph{audiometric hearing loss} was weaker and
did not reach significance (OR 1.18, 0.99--1.41); there, occupational noise (OR
1.72, 1.44--2.06), age (OR 1.13 per year), and male sex (female sex OR 0.47,
0.40--0.55) dominated. These are design-based associations, not causal effects.

\paragraph{Interpretation (H1 supported).} This is precisely the prespecified
pattern of H1, now demonstrated in the primary cohort with the \emph{actual}
perceived-stress exposure: perceived stress tracks the tinnitus \emph{symptom}
(OR 1.42, $p<0.001$) but attenuates against the audiometric \emph{threshold}
(OR 1.18, $p=0.07$) once occupational noise and age are accounted for. The
symptom-versus-threshold dissociation seen preliminarily in NHANES (with a
distress proxy) thus holds for the genuine stress exposure in KNHANES, consistent
with a stress--tinnitus association that is not primarily explained by measurable
cochlear threshold shift.

\paragraph{Extended and sensitivity models.} To test whether the primary
stress--tinnitus association is an artifact of under-adjustment, the same
design-based pipeline fits prespecified secondary specifications that isolate the
perceived-stress odds ratio (Table~\ref{tab:results_knhanes_extended}): an
\emph{extended} model adding the mediator depressed mood (interpreted as a
mediator-adjusted, not confounder-adjusted, estimate); a model additionally
adjusting for \emph{audiometric hearing loss} (a strong tinnitus correlate); and
alternative hearing-loss outcome definitions---\emph{worse-ear} speech-frequency
PTA (sensitive to the unilateral/asymmetric presentations most relevant to the
SSNHL motivation) and \emph{high-frequency} PTA at 3/4/6\,kHz (where
noise-related damage appears first). These models are generated by
\texttt{code/src/knhanes\_analysis.py} (\texttt{-{}-latex-extended}); rows not yet
populated require the KDCA-approved microdata run, which regenerates the table
in place.
\IfFileExists{tables/table_extended_knhanes.tex}{\input{tables/table_extended_knhanes}}{}

\paragraph{Audiometric protocol and conductive contamination.} The KNHANES ENT
examination provides pure-tone \emph{air-conduction} thresholds per ear; the
better-ear speech-frequency PTA is the primary hearing-loss definition, with the
worse-ear and high-frequency definitions above as prespecified sensitivity
analyses. Because air-conduction thresholds alone cannot separate conductive from
sensorineural loss, any conductive component is a source of outcome
misclassification; the availability of tympanometry/bone-conduction data to
exclude conductive patterns in these cycles is confirmed against the KNHANES
user guide, and where such data are absent, conductive contamination is
treated as a limitation (Discussion) rather than silently ignored.

\paragraph{Further prospective analyses.} The employed-restricted
(healthy-worker) contrast, the KNHANES$\rightarrow$NHANES external validation
(calibration, recalibration, common support, case-mix standardization; H2), and
subgroup fairness (SES; race/ethnicity in NHANES) are prospective components that
follow the prespecified plan (Methods and Section~\ref{sec:ai}); they carry no
results here.

\subsection{Reproducible scaffold demonstration (synthetic; not a result)}
To show that the pipeline runs end-to-end and produces correctly structured, TRIPOD+AI-aligned outputs, the repository ships a synthetic-data demonstration. \textbf{These numbers are artifacts of randomly generated data and carry no epidemiological meaning}; they exist only to verify wiring, metric computation, and reporting format, and are deliberately kept out of the abstract and any headline. On the synthetic sample the tinnitus target yields AUROC~$\approx$~0.64, AUPRC~$\approx$~0.49, Brier~$\approx$~0.20, and the hearing-loss target yields an analogous record (Table~\ref{tab:metrics}). Substituting real KNHANES/NHANES files following the documented layout routes the identical code path to the design-consistent estimates specified above.

\subsection{Preliminary real-data results (NHANES public-use)}
Using the tested, design-based pipeline (\texttt{code/src/nhanes\_analysis.py};
estimators unit-tested in \texttt{code/src/test\_nhanes\_analysis.py}), we
analyzed real NHANES public-use files for U.S. adults aged 40--69, pooling the
2011--2012, 2015--2016, and 2017--2018 cycles (Table~\ref{tab:results}).
\IfFileExists{tables/table_results.tex}{\input{tables/table_results}}{}

\paragraph{Prevalence.} Survey-weighted prevalence was 28.5\% (95\% CI 26.5--30.4;
$n=5{,}354$) for any tinnitus in the past 12 months, 12.0\% (10.4--13.6;
$n=4{,}808$) for better-ear speech-frequency hearing loss ($>$25 dB HL), and 8.4\%
(7.5--9.4; $n=7{,}293$) for a positive PHQ-9 depression screen.

\paragraph{Associations.} In mutually adjusted design-based logistic models
(adjusting for occupational noise, depressed mood, age, and sex), \emph{tinnitus}
was associated with occupational-noise exposure (OR 1.59, 1.32--1.92), depressed
mood (OR 1.61, 1.23--2.11), and female sex (OR 1.61, 1.35--1.91), but not with age
(OR 1.00, 0.98--1.01). \emph{Hearing loss} was associated with age (OR 1.11 per
year, 1.09--1.13) and depressed mood (OR 2.29, 1.56--3.35), inversely with female
sex (OR 0.42, 0.29--0.61), and only weakly and non-significantly with occupational
noise (OR 1.22, 0.94--1.58). These are associations, not causal effects.

\paragraph{Interpretation (a priori pattern).} The pattern is coherent with the
framework's hypotheses. The psychological-distress proxy is associated with both
outcomes, but more than doubles the odds of \emph{audiometric} hearing loss while
showing a smaller association with \emph{tinnitus}; age dominates hearing loss but
is unrelated to tinnitus in this band; and the sex pattern (higher tinnitus,
lower hearing loss in women) matches known epidemiology. Because the proxy is a
mediator in the DAG, these are mediator-level associations, and NHANES lacks the
perceived-stress exposure; they therefore motivate---but do not substitute
for---the primary KNHANES stress analysis. All values are reproduced by the
committed results file and can be regenerated end-to-end from the CDC files.

\subsection{Planned AI-component reporting}
LLM-extracted fields will be reported with the per-field metric suite of Section~\ref{sec:ai} (exact match; precision/recall/F1; omission; span agreement; temporal/laterality/onset/duration accuracy; document-level match; run consistency; clinically significant error rate), plus clinician correction time and per-version performance. The red-flag layer will be reported per Table~\ref{tab:redflag} (dual-expert-labeled vignettes; sensitivity with 95\% CI; specificity/over-referral; subgroup and robustness), with any missed red flag audited as a critical failure and with the explicit caveat that finite-set perfection does not guarantee zero deployment misses. Model cards accompany each released model, documenting intended use (Table~\ref{tab:intended}), data provenance, subgroup metrics, and prohibitions.
